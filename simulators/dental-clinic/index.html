Insurance & Accreditation

Maximize your dental benefits. Accredited with PhilHealth YAKAP and major HMO networks. No surprises.

🏥 PhilHealth YAKAP Accredited

YAKAP provides ₱1,000 per beneficiary per year for preventive oral health services. We file directly on your behalf; zero paperwork on your end.

  • Oral examination & consultation: covered
  • Professional cleaning (prophylaxis): covered
  • Fluoride application: covered
  • Dental sealant application: covered
  • Direct claim filing: we handle all paperwork
  • Valid for members and qualified dependents

Perlas Dental Clinic was founded with a single mission: to make professional dental care accessible to every family in Aurora Province. We believe a healthy smile is the foundation for confidence, health, and well-being; not a luxury.

Led by Dr. Maricel Santos, our clinic combines the warmth of provincial healthcare with evidence-based practice. We stay current with continuing education, modern equipment, and best-in-class materials. Your smile deserves nothing less.

We are proud to be among the very few clinics in Aurora Province accredited with PhilHealth YAKAP and major HMO networks, ensuring dental care is as accessible as possible.
