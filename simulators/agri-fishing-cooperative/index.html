Who We Are

Built by farmers and
fisherfolk, for each other.

Sagisag Aurora Multipurpose Cooperative was organized in 2009 by a small group of rice farmers and fishing families in Baler who pooled their savings to escape predatory lending — the informal "5-6" loans that kept many households trapped in debt during lean seasons.

Today SAMC serves over 1,280 members across Aurora Province with savings and loan products, a produce marketing arm that buys members' harvest at fair, published prices, and an agri-supply store offering seeds, fertilizer, and fishing gear at member discounts. Every member is a part-owner — and every peso of surplus is returned to members as patronage refunds and dividends.

Join the Cooperative

Become a member-owner
in four simple steps.

Membership is open to residents of Aurora Province who are at least 18 years old. As a member, you become a part-owner of the cooperative — with voting rights, access to savings and loan products, and a share in annual surplus through patronage refunds.

1

Attend a Pre-Membership Education Seminar (PMES)

A half-day orientation covering cooperative principles, member rights and responsibilities, and SAMC's products and services. Held monthly at the Baler main office and rotating barangay venues.

2

Submit Your Membership Application

Valid government ID, proof of residency in Aurora Province, 1x1 photo, and a completed application form (available below or at any branch).

3

Pay Your Share Capital Contribution

A minimum initial share capital of ₱1,000 (payable in installments) plus a one-time ₱100 membership fee. Share capital earns annual dividends based on cooperative performance.

4

Activate Your Member Account

Once approved by the Board, your membership is activated — you can open a savings account, apply for loans, and sell produce through the marketing program immediately.
