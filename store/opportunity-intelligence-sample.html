COMMISSIONING SAMPLE — FICTIONAL BUSINESS

Opportunity Intelligence Brief

Target: Harbor & Pine Home Services — fictional local service company. This sample demonstrates product structure only. All company details and scores below are invented for commissioning and are not claims about a real business.

Decision summary: The highest-leverage move is not a website redesign. The visible bottleneck is lead capture and follow-up: demand can arrive, but the operating path from inquiry to owner action is weak.

Opportunity map

Digital Presence
7/10
Credible service information and current contact details.
Lead Capture
3/10
Contact exists, but source, status, next action, and follow-up are not visible.
Customer Experience
6/10
The service promise is understandable; response expectations are unclear.
Search Visibility
5/10
Branded/local discovery exists; high-intent service coverage is incomplete.
Operational Systems
3/10
Public evidence cannot verify an operating ledger; buyer context says follow-up is manual.
Revenue Expansion
7/10
Repeat service and referral paths appear plausible but are not systematized.

Top three priorities

01 — Establish a lead ledger
Capture every inquiry with source, status, owner, next action, and follow-up date. Expected outcome: fewer lost opportunities and clearer response accountability.
02 — Define the response path
Standardize acknowledgment, qualification, quote handoff, and follow-up. Expected outcome: faster response and lower owner-memory dependence.
03 — Expand high-intent service visibility
Once lead handling is stable, improve pages around services buyers already search for. Expected outcome: more discoverable demand entering a system capable of handling it.

30-day action sequence

Week 1: define lead fields and create one operating ledger.
Week 2: route every public inquiry into the ledger and set response ownership.
Week 3: measure response time, follow-up completion, and lost-lead reasons.
Week 4: use the first operating data to decide whether the next investment belongs in search visibility, review generation, or automation.

What remains unknown

Actual conversion rate, lifetime value, response-time baseline, lead-source mix, and repeat-purchase rate are not inferable from public evidence. A real brief would mark these unknown unless the buyer provides them.

Commissioning verdict

The product demonstrates the intended value: prioritize the bottleneck before prescribing implementation. The commercial hypothesis remains unproven until external buyers pay for and use the brief.
