Revenue Leak Audit

Example target: a UK cleaning company serving commercial and residential customers. This review uses only the public homepage as it appeared in September 2026. The company name and URL are withheld; no analytics, credentials, or private customer data were used.

Highest-leverage finding: the homepage makes visitors learn about the company before helping them choose the right buying path. Commercial and residential customers enter through the same generic opening, so the first decision is slower than it needs to be.

What the visitor sees now

FIRST SCREEN

Company-first opening

The page opens with a welcome message, a broad “commercial & domestic cleaning” label, and a paragraph describing the company and its range of services.

SERVICES

Many offers at once

Construction, hotel, office, commercial, domestic, and residential services appear before the visitor has been clearly segmented.

PROOF

Useful trust exists

Testimonials, UK coverage, multiple offices, flexibility, and service claims give the business material to build confidence.

ACTION

Quote path arrives late

A phone number and quote form exist, but the homepage does not turn the first screen into a clean commercial-vs-residential decision.

Revenue leak map

01 — Clarity: mixed audiences

Commercial facilities buyers and residential customers have different needs, proof thresholds, and buying cycles. Treating them as one audience forces both to filter the page for relevance.

02 — Action: no dominant first decision

The visitor can eventually call or request a quote, but the opening should answer a simpler question first: Which service path are you here for?

03 — Trust: proof is present but underused

The site already has testimonials and geographic coverage. Moving one strong proof signal close to the primary CTA would reduce uncertainty earlier.

04 — Friction: service breadth becomes decision load

A long list of capabilities proves range, but breadth before segmentation makes scanning harder. Route first; expand services second.

05 — Follow-through: unknown from the public site

The homepage cannot prove how quickly quote requests are answered, who owns the lead, or what happens after submission. Those are operational questions to verify internally, not assumptions to invent.

Recommended first screen

REWRITE

Professional cleaning for workplaces and homes across the UK.

Choose the service you need, request a no-obligation quote, and get routed to the right cleaning team without hunting through the site.

Supporting proof line: UK coverage · Flexible service plans · Commercial & residential teams

Priority fixes

  1. Split the first decision. Give commercial and residential visitors distinct paths above the fold.
  2. Make one CTA dominant. If commercial contracts are the higher-value objective, make the commercial quote the primary action and residential the secondary route.
  3. Move proof upward. Place one testimonial or coverage/trust statement directly beneath the CTA.
  4. Reduce service-list noise. Show service categories after segmentation rather than asking every visitor to process the full catalog.
  5. Define the quote handoff. Tell buyers what happens after submission: response window, next step, and whether a site survey is required.

7-day repair sequence

DAY 1
Choose the primary buyer.

Confirm whether commercial contracts, residential jobs, or both equally drive the business.

DAY 2
Replace the hero.

Install the segmented opening and one primary CTA.

DAY 3
Route service paths.

Give each audience its own relevant services and proof.

DAY 4
Lift trust signals.

Move the strongest testimonial, coverage statement, and legitimacy cues higher.

DAY 5
Tighten the quote form.

Keep only fields needed to qualify and respond to the lead.

DAY 6
Mobile QA.

Check the first screen, CTA visibility, phone action, form usability, and service routing on a small screen.

DAY 7
Measure the handoff.

Track quote submissions and phone clicks, then compare performance after the changes.

What this audit does not claim

Without analytics we cannot claim the current conversion rate, traffic quality, abandonment rate, or revenue impact of any single change. The audit identifies observable buyer-journey friction and gives a prioritized repair plan; measured results require implementation and tracking.
