## Recommended Page Sections / Nav Structure
- **Home** — hero: split CTA framing ("Residential" / "Commercial") above
  the fold, region served (San Luis & Aurora Province), emergency contact
  prominent (mobile-first, tap-to-call), "no water / burst pipe" urgency cue
- **Services** — segmented grid: Residential (leak repair, drain unclogging,
  water heater install/repair, bathroom/kitchen fixture installation, septic
  tank pumping, water pressure/repiping) vs. Commercial (commercial fixture
  installation, grease-trap and drainage maintenance contracts, backflow
  prevention/compliance inspections, facility maintenance retainers)
- **Emergency Service** — dedicated CTA block: 24/7 burst-pipe / no-water /
  sewage-backup response, response-time promise (mirrors Brightline's
  emergency band for cross-portfolio consistency)
- **Projects / Gallery** — before/after pipe repairs, bathroom renovations,
  commercial installs
- **About / Licensing** — licensed master plumbers, years operating, safety
  & sanitation certifications
- **Service Area** — San Luis + Aurora Province map/list (local SEO anchor
  page, reuse Brightline's service-area pattern)
- **Contact / Get a Quote** — lead form (Layer 2) with service dropdown
  incl. "Emergency / No Water / Burst Pipe", routes to operator inbox
- **Operator Portal (gated, `<archetype>2026` convention → `plumber2026`)**
  — Job/Ticket Tracking (residential vs. commercial queue, emergency flag),
  Maintenance Contract tracker (recurring commercial clients — MRR signal),
  Business Intelligence (jobs by category, avg response time, revenue by
  service line), Marketing Generator (rainy-season pipe-stress / flooding
  prep push, septic maintenance reminders, bathroom-renovation seasonal
  promo, post-job review request), AI Console (pipeline intelligence,
  overdue maintenance contracts / MRR risk flags, emergency SLA status)

## Brand Direction
- **Palette:** Deep teal/aqua (#0E7C86) + clean white + slate gray (#3A3F44)
  + warm amber accent (#F2A93B) for CTAs — water/clean-utility association
  without overlapping Brightline's electric-blue/safety-yellow or
  IronCrest's industrial orange/steel. Distinct, recognizable as "the water
  trade" at a glance in the portfolio grid.
- **Fonts:** Headings — "Barlow Semi Condensed" (shared with Brightline for
  cross-portfolio trade-family consistency); Body/UI — "Source Sans 3"
  (shared with Brightline).
- **Tone:** Reliable, fast-response, sanitation-conscious. "Fixed right the
  first time" framing. Short sentences, strong tap-to-call CTAs, emphasis on
  licensing for commercial credibility and rapid response for residential
  emergencies (burst pipe / no water / sewage backup).
