# Build Brief — 2026-06-16

## Archetype: Trades Contractor — Niche Variant (Class B — Revenue)
**Niche: Residential & Commercial Aircon / HVAC Contractor**

### Why this niche, why today
Rotation sequence confirmed: Trades Contractor niche variants → electrician ✓ (2026-06-14,
Brightline Electrical Services) → plumber ✓ (2026-06-15, ClearFlow Plumbing Services) → HVAC
→ roofing. Today's slot is **HVAC / Aircon Contractor**.

"HVAC" is the trade-industry label; "aircon" is the dominant PH-market search term — the
build should lean into "aircon" for all user-facing copy and SEO targets while using HVAC
internally/on operator side to signal technical authority.

Aircon is one of the highest-demand residential and commercial services in the Philippines
year-round (tropical climate, no seasonal dead period unlike temperate-market HVAC), which
means the Foundry can pitch this demo to any PH SMB owner running a cooling business without
"seasonality" objections. Strong recurring-revenue model (preventive maintenance contracts =
predictable MRR — ideal Class B framing for the client conversation).

Distinct from Electrician (power systems) and Plumber (water/sanitation): zero portfolio
cannibalization. Three trades, three different emergency service pitches:
- Electrician → power outage / panel failure
- Plumber → burst pipe / no water
- Aircon → unit failure during summer peak / commercial downtime risk

## Recommended Page Sections / Nav Structure

**Nav:** FrostLine Aircon | Services | Residential | Commercial | Service Area | Contact
(+ Emergency band under nav — tap-to-call, 24/7 aircon emergency)

**Home**
- Hero: split CTA "Residential Aircon" / "Commercial Aircon" — region served (San Luis &
  Aurora Province), 24/7 emergency number prominent (mobile tap-to-call)
- Trust band: years operating, units serviced, brands supported, response time promise

- Split-type installation (wall-mount, window-type, multi-split)
- Aircon cleaning & chemical wash
- Repair & troubleshooting
- Freon refill / refrigerant recharge
- Annual / quarterly maintenance plans (upsell to recurring)
- Emergency same-day service (highest-intent CTA)

**Commercial Services**
- Ceiling cassette, floor/ceiling mounted, ducted, VRF systems
- Commercial PM contracts (quarterly/annual — recurring revenue framing)
- New fit-out coordination (project-based commercial installs)
- Multi-unit fleet maintenance for hotels, offices, retail
- Response SLA for commercial clients (downtime risk framing)

**Emergency Service** — dedicated section/band
- 24/7 rapid response, what qualifies, response-time promise
- "Aircon failure during summer peak = revenue loss for your business" — B2B urgency angle

**Projects / Gallery** — residential installs, commercial cassette installs, before/after cleans

**About / Certifications** — TESDA-certified aircon technicians, brands serviced (Carrier,
Daikin, Midea, Panasonic, Fujitsu, Samsung, LG, Sharp, Condura), years in Aurora Province

**Service Area** — San Luis + coverage map: Baler, Maria Aurora, Dingalan, Casiguran, Dipaculao,
Dilasag, Dinalungan (full Aurora Province municipality list — local SEO anchor)

**Contact / Get a Quote** — lead form (type of unit, issue description, residential/commercial,
preferred schedule) → operator inbox

**Operator Portal (gated `/operator.html`, gate code `frostline2026`)**
- Job / Ticket Tracking: service type (install/clean/repair/PM), unit brand, residential vs
  commercial flag, schedule date, status
- PM Contract Tracker: commercial clients on recurring maintenance, contract renewal dates,
  revenue per contract (MRR signal)
- Business Intelligence: jobs by service type, avg ticket value, revenue by service line
  (installation vs cleaning vs PM), seasonal peak detector
- Marketing Generator: seasonal push templates (summer peak "is your aircon ready?", back-to-
  school unit-check promos, commercial "don't lose a weekend of sales to a broken aircon" push)
- AI Console: diagnostics — PM contracts due for renewal, overdue service calls, top revenue
  service lines this month, technician utilization rate

## Brand Direction

**Palette:**
- Ice Blue primary: `#1B8FCC` (cool, clean, water-like — temperature signal)
- Steel/Silver accent: `#8EAFC4` (technical precision, equipment feel)
- Dark Navy base: `#0D2236` (depth, professionalism, high contrast for text)
- White/Off-white: `#F4F9FC` (clean, airy — cooling association)
- Alert accent: `#E84040` (emergency band only — hot contrast against the cool palette)

**Distinct from existing trades:**
- IronCrest (trades-contractor): orange/steel/charcoal → construction/heavy
- Brightline (electrician): electric blue + safety yellow → power/caution
- ClearFlow (plumbing): navy + copper/amber → water/pipe
- **FrostLine (aircon): ice blue + steel + dark navy → cold air, technical, clinical**

**Fonts:**
- Headings: "Barlow Semi Condensed" (carry forward from plumbing/electrician — trade family
  consistency; also highly legible on mobile)
- Body/UI: "Source Sans 3" (carry forward — proven readable for service lists and forms)
- Mono accents (operator, stats): "JetBrains Mono" (carry forward — token system continuity)

**Tone:**
Cool, precise, reliable. "Always cool. Always on." framing. Emphasis on:
- Rapid response (same-day / emergency)
- Certified technicians (TESDA — trust signal for PH residential clients)
- Recurring PM contracts for commercial clients (reliability over the long haul)
- Seasonal urgency ("summer peak" in PH runs March–May; frame emergency service around it
  even if the build ships mid-year)
