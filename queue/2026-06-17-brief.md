# Build Brief — 2026-06-17

## Archetype: Trades Contractor — Niche Variant (Class B — Revenue)
**Niche: Residential & Commercial Roofing Contractor**

### Why this niche, why today
Trades Contractor niche rotation: electrician ✓ (Brightline, 2026-06-14) → plumber ✓
(ClearFlow, 2026-06-15) → HVAC/Aircon ✓ (FrostLine, 2026-06-16) → **Roofing** (today).

Roofing is the 4th and final Trades niche variant before the rotation advances to Professional
Services variants. In the Philippines, roofing is a perennial high-demand trade driven by two
forces no other trade shares: (1) annual typhoon season (June–November) — the Philippines sits
squarely in the Western Pacific typhoon belt, and roof damage is the single most common
property consequence of storm events; (2) hot/wet climate degradation — rust, moss, warped
sheets, and failed waterproofing are chronic issues for corrugated metal roofing (the
dominant residential roofing type in rural and provincial areas). This creates a dual
demand engine: emergency/post-typhoon repair AND preventive maintenance/replacement.

Distinct from all existing trades archetypes — no service overlap:
- IronCrest (trades-contractor): general construction/renovation → structural/interior
- Brightline (electrician): power systems → electrical
- ClearFlow (plumber): water/sanitation → piping
- FrostLine (aircon): cooling systems → HVAC equipment
- **ApexGuard (roofing): building envelope protection → roofing/waterproofing** ← new

Aurora Province is rural-provincial Philippines, meaning metal roofing dominates (Long Span,
corrugated GI, stone-coated). A well-structured roofing contractor site here faces near-zero
direct online competition — opportunity gap is wide.
