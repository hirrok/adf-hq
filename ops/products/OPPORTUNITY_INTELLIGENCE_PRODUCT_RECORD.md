# Opportunity Intelligence — Product Record

**Product ID:** ADF-PROD-OI-001
**State:** VALIDATION
**Mode:** Paid pilot
**Effective:** 2026-09-27
**Owner:** Aurora Digital Foundry

## Target user
Owner-operated and small businesses that know something is underperforming but do not yet know which digital or operational intervention deserves priority.

## Problem
Small operators receive disconnected advice about websites, SEO, social media, CRM, automation, reviews, and AI. The decision problem is choosing the highest-leverage intervention now.

## Product hypothesis
A compact evidence-led assessment that maps six operating dimensions and converts them into a priority-ranked action plan can improve decision quality enough that buyers will pay for diagnosis before implementation.

## Validation offer
**Opportunity Intelligence Brief — Founding Pilot**
**Validation price:** USD 79 one-time.

This is a test price, not a permanent canonical list price.

### Buyer provides
- business name,
- public website or primary public business URL,
- primary business outcome to improve,
- short context on the perceived bottleneck,
- optional public profiles.

No passwords, customer data, analytics access, or private credentials are required.

### Deliverable
- executive summary,
- observable current-state findings,
- six-dimension opportunity map:
  - Digital Presence
  - Lead Capture
  - Customer Experience
  - Search Visibility
  - Operational Systems
  - Revenue Expansion Potential
- explicit unknowns where evidence is insufficient,
- top three priority interventions,
- expected business outcome for each,
- 30-day action sequence,
- implementation options separated from diagnosis,
- PDF delivery by email.

## Exclusions
No implementation, guaranteed traffic/leads/rankings/revenue/savings/ROI, private analytics access, regulated advice, persistent SaaS workspace, recurring monitoring, or live integrations.

## Validation milestone
Remain in VALIDATION until external paid evidence exists.

**Continue / expand:** at least 3 paid external orders, deliverables completed, no material scope confusion, buyers confirm improved prioritization or decision quality, and fulfillment effort remains economically viable.

**Reprice / redesign:** interest exists but buyers resist USD 79, request materially different output, or fulfillment effort is too high.

**Park:** no paid demand after a reasonable distribution test, or buyers consistently prefer the Website Conversion Audit.

## Production path after validation
1. persistent assessment records,
2. automated report generation,
3. saved comparisons / reassessment,
4. recurring monitoring only if usage supports recurrence,
5. business-owned data integrations,
6. productized implementation packages.

Do not build accounts, databases, subscriptions, or live AI integrations before the paid-pilot gate.

## Revenue hypothesis
Phase 1: one-time paid diagnostic.
Phase 2: diagnostic → implementation engagement.
Phase 3: optional recurring reassessment / intelligence if recurrence is proven.

## Success event
A buyer pays for the pilot, receives the brief, and can identify what to do next without purchasing implementation.
